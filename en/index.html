The two pillars

Compassion and communion : the heart of this vocation

Compassion: contemplating the sorrows of Mary, bending down to the misery of others, having mercy on them, drawing near to those who suffer.

Communion: living together as brothers, in a single family, sharing prayer, meals and work.

Two pillars lived in the seraphic simplicity of Saint Francis of Assisi, to embody the Gospel of Jesus Christ today.

Our path in the Church

The experience began in 2016 as a small group, driven by the desire to live the Franciscan life together in compassion and communion. Since 2019 the friars have been recognised as an association of the faithful in the diocese of Fréjus-Toulon, where they carry out their life of prayer and apostolate.
